PGC Digital Innovation Challenge

The PGC Digital Innovation Challenge is a technology and innovation

competition spearheaded by the Provincial Government of Cagayan (PGC),

under Provincial Administrators’ Office.

This challenge provides an opportunity for college students and fresh graduates

to develop and showcase innovative digital solutions that contribute to

strengthening provincial governance through the EGAY Platform, fostering

efficient, transparent, inclusive, and technology-driven public service delivery.

Date of Presentation: December 10, 2026

Time: 9:00 o’clock AM. to 4:00 P.M.

Venue: SP Session Hall, PPDO Building, PGC, Capitol Compound, Caggay,

Tuguegarao City, Cagayan

Participants may propose innovations such as:

- Information Systems and Web/Mobile Applications
- Internet of Things (IoT) solutions
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications
- Data analytics and dashboards
- Smart devices and automation
- Other emerging digital technologies applicable to local government

1. Purpose

- Generate practical digital innovations that can be adopted by PGC.
- Address real-world needs of the province, its towns, and barangays.
- Encourage collaboration between the academe, professionals, and

government.

- Promote and advance the core principles of the EGAY Platform through

practical, technology-driven solutions for the Province of Cagayan.

2\. Who may participate

o Participants must be either:

(a) officially enrolled college students in any recognized college or

university within the Province of Cagayan, (b) graduates of any recognized

college or university in the Province of Cagayan belonging to the Class of

2026\. Applicants from Information Technology (IT), Engineering, and other

related disciplines are eligible to participate, provided that their proposed

project is technology-driven and aligned with the objectives of the

challenge.

o All participants must be permanent residents of any city or municipality

within the Province of Cagayan.

o Each school may submit multiple entries to the competition.

o Each team must be composed of three (3) members.

o Each team must have a faculty adviser who will provide guidance

throughout the competition.

o A formal recommendation letter from the College Dean (or an authorized

academic official) must be submitted as part of the application

requirements.

o Mixed teams composed of students/graduates and or non-students are

not allowed under this category.

3\. Thematic Areas

All participating projects must clearly demonstrate the application of technology

to solve problems or create opportunities within at least one of the following

EGAY pillars:

- Education: Innovations that enhance learning, improve classroom

accessibility, or digitize educational resources.

- Good Governance: Tools that promote transparency, streamline

government services, or improve citizen engagement.

- Agriculture: High-tech solutions for crop monitoring, smart irrigation, pest

control, or supply chain efficiency.

- Youth & Elderly: Gadgets or systems designed for the safety, health, and

social well-being of the youngest and oldest members of our community.

Projects may cover additional local government concerns (e.g.,

disaster preparedness, environment, tourism, infrastructure), but they must

clearly anchor their primary impact in at least one of the four EGAY pillars

above.

5\. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (IP) OWNERSHIP AND UTILIZATION

- The primary objective of this competition is to generate innovative digital

solutions and systems that may be adopted, implemented, enhanced, or

scaled for use by the Provincial Government of Cagayan (PGC) and,

where applicable, other Local Government Units (LGUs).

- All participating teams shall retain ownership of the intellectual property

rights to their submitted projects prior to winning the competition.

- Winning projects, including the Champion and Runners-Up, shall grant the

Provincial Government of Cagayan a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free,

and non-exclusive right to:

o Use and deploy the system or solution;

o Modify, enhance, improve, or further develop the project;

o Integrate the solution into existing or future government systems;

o Share or replicate the solution with other LGUs for public-sector use.

- Winning teams acknowledge that their projects are intended for public

service application and therefore shall not submit the same project, in

whole or in substantial part, to any similar innovation, hackathon, or digital

solution competition after being declared a winner under this challenge.

- The PGC shall have the right to continue development or improvement of

the winning solutions, either independently or through third parties, without

additional consent or compensation beyond the prizes awarded, provided

that such use remains aligned with public governance and noncommercial purposes.

- For non-winning entries, participants grant the PGC a non-exclusive,

royalty-free license to use submitted materials solely for documentation,

research, reference, and promotional purposes, without affecting the

team’s right to further develop or commercialize their projects.

- Any commercial use, transfer, or licensing of a winning project outside

government or public-sector purposes shall be subject to a separate

written agreement between the PGC and the project owners.

7\. ORIGINALITY, RESEARCH, AND RESPONSIBLE USE OF AI

- Originality of the Proposed Solution

All proposed solutions, concepts, designs, system features, methodologies, and

other outputs submitted for the PGC Digital Innovation Challenge must be the

original work of the participating team.

Participants may use existing technologies, frameworks, references, and

publicly available information as appropriate; however, the proposed solution,

its application, design, and implementation must be developed by the

team and must not be copied, reproduced, or substantially derived from another

person's or organization's work without proper acknowledgment.

Any submission found to contain plagiarized, copied, fabricated, or substantially

non-original work may be disqualified from the competition.

- Review of Related Literature / References

Where a Review of Related Literature (RRL), related studies, research,

technical references, or other supporting sources are included in the project

write-up, these shall be used to establish the credibility, relevance, and basis

of the proposed solution.

All sources must be properly and accurately cited and referenced using an

appropriate and consistent citation style.

Any reference found to be fabricated, unverifiable, improperly attributed, or

intentionally misrepresented may be grounds for disqualification of the

submitted work.

- Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools

The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools is allowed only as a support or

enhancement tool in the development of the project.

Teams may use AI tools for purposes such as:

- brainstorming and idea generation;
- improving grammar, spelling, and language;
- organizing or refining content;
- assisting with coding or debugging;
- generating design suggestions; and
- other similar tasks that support the team's own work.

The following are not allowed:

- Submitting an AI-generated solution as the team's own original work;
- Relying primarily or entirely on AI to develop the project or its major

components;

- Submitting AI-generated RRL, research findings, or references without

independently verifying and properly citing the actual sources;

- Using AI to fabricate data, findings, citations, references, or other

supporting information; and

- Misrepresenting AI-generated content as the team's own research,

analysis, or original work.
