Brand Identity, Version 1, October 29, 2026

Entirety of Cagayan’s Finance and Performance, seen, centralized, and managed, in a single platform.

We present to you, Kita. Cagayan’s finances come into focus first. Its businesses, its people, and its future come next.

1 Name

Kita

A Filipino word that carries the whole product in four letters. It is the one word that holds the finances, the registry, and the public view.

EarnedKita means income and profit. It stands for the government finances and the businesses the platform records.
SeenKita also means clearly visible, as in kitang-kita. It stands for transparency: figures anyone can see, with their source.
SharedKita is spoken from one person to another, as in mahal kita. It stands for a government speaking openly to its people, and the people answering back through their wishes.
2 The Mark

An Eye Made of Data

A halftone eye holds a small magnifying lens as its iris. The eye is the public looking. The lens is the detail: one town, one barangay, one figure. The brass dot at its center is the pupil, the single thing you chose to look at. The same brass dot becomes the dot on the i in the wordmark.

Dots

57 dots on a 10 unit grid, all one size, exactly as the map draws them. Depth carries the value, never size.

Depth

The five map shades. The deepest dots sit closest to the lens, so the eye focuses where you look.

Brass

Used only for the pupil and the dot on the i. It marks the chosen place, as it does on the map.

3 Logo

Lockups

The horizontal lockup is primary. Use the stacked lockup for square or narrow spaces, the mark alone for avatars and stamps, and the icon for the browser tab and the app. Reverse versions go only on River ink or another dark surface. The one color version, kita-mark-mono.svg, takes any single color through currentColor for print, stamps and watermarks.

4 Space and Size

Clear Space and Minimum Size

  • Keep clear space on every side equal to half the height of the letter K (about one fourteenth of the lockup width).
  • Horizontal lockup: never below 96 px wide, or 25 mm in print.
  • Mark alone: never below 32 px wide. Smaller than that, use the icon.
  • Icon: works down to 16 px.

6 Typography

Geist, the Same Voice as the Product

Wordmark Kita Geist SemiBold, outlined in the logo files, with a round brass dot on the i.
Tagline Cagayan, Seen Cagayan, Seen Geist Mono, uppercase and tracked, as the app's labels, on title cards and covers. Geist SemiBold in Title Case where it reads as a sentence.
  • The name is always written Kita. Never KITA or kita.
  • Do not retype the wordmark in another font. Use the logo files.
  • Titles and headings carry no commas, dashes, middots or pipes, as in the product.
7 Usage

Do Not

Stretch or squash the logo.
Place it on colors outside the palette, or on photographs.
Recolor any part, including the brass.
Add shadows, glows, gradients or outlines.
Rotate or tilt the mark.
Use the light logo on a dark surface. Use the reverse logo.

No gradients anywhere, as in the product. The dots stay one size. Brass is never used for decoration, buttons, or links.
